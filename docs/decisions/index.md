# Architectural Decision Records (ADRs)

> Read this index to understand foundational architectural choices, their trade-offs, and historical context.

## ADR Protocol

- Architectural Decision Records are numbered sequentially and are **permanent**.
- Once recorded, ADR numbers must not be reused or renumbered.
- If a future decision replaces or modifies an earlier decision, the new ADR must mark the previous ADR as `Superseded by ADR-XXX` and provide links.
