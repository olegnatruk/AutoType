# AutoType

AutoType is a free, account-free Chrome extension for people who want a prepared passage entered gradually at their chosen caret, instead of inserted all at once. The first release targets Google Docs and ordinary website text fields. Its audience and workflow come from the user's approved implementation plan of 2026-10-05.

## Workflow and success

Paste a plain-text draft into the popup, close it, click the destination, and press the start/pause/resume shortcut. The passage is entered with a natural rhythm averaging approximately 75 WPM. Stop, focus changes, and navigation must prevent unwanted continuation. Pasted drafts stay on the device until cleared; the chosen website receives the entered text.

## Business model and boundaries

Public, free release. No account, backend, analytics, payments, or cloud sync. Browser debugger permission and Chrome's visible debugging banner are accepted. First delivery includes a testable MVP, project memory, and store submission materials; publication needs a publisher account, a hosted privacy-policy URL, and final approval. Complex editors beyond Docs and ordinary fields are outside v1.

## Compatibility claims

Google Docs is an implementation target, not a verified claim until a live extension test passes. Destination autoformatting and collaborator update batching are controlled by the website. See [verification](docs/VERIFICATION.md) for actual results and remaining release gates.
