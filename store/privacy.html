AutoType privacy policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026.

What AutoType handles

AutoType handles the plain text you paste into its popup, the chosen destination tab and editor, and typing progress. It uses that information to enter your passage at your selected cursor and to pause or stop when focus changes.

Storage and retention

Your saved passage is stored in Chrome's local extension storage on your device until you clear it or uninstall AutoType. Active and paused run details are stored in Chrome's session storage, removed when the run finishes or stops, and discarded when the browser restarts. AutoType does not encrypt this storage itself. It does not use Chrome Sync.

Where your text goes

AutoType sends your passage as keyboard/text input to the website you choose. That website may save, process, or share the entered text under its own policies and document-sharing settings. AutoType does not send your passage or browsing data to its publisher or an analytics service. It has no backend, account system, advertising, or tracking SDK.

Browser access

AutoType uses debugger access to send browser-level input, storage access to save drafts, and temporary active-tab/scripting access to inspect the selected editor and stop on focus changes. Chrome displays a debugging banner during active typing. AutoType does not use these capabilities to inspect network traffic or collect browsing history.

Your controls

Use Clear passage to remove the saved passage and stop its run. Stop cancels the run but leaves the saved draft available. Uninstalling removes the extension's stored data. Clearing AutoType cannot remove text already entered into another website; use that website's controls.

Contact and changes

Use the support contact listed on AutoType's Chrome Web Store listing for privacy questions. The publisher must configure that contact before public release. Updates to data handling will be reflected in this policy and, where required, the extension interface and store disclosures.
